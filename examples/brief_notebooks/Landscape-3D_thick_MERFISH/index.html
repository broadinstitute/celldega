    Cell size and continuous rotation¶

    cell_size sets the default cell diameter in microns, assuming the centroid coordinates are in microns. Change cell_size=10.0 below and rerun the notebook to try another default.

    In the interactive preview, the CELL slider multiplies this diameter: its midpoint is 1× (10 µm here), the minimum is 0×, and the maximum is 2× (20 µm here). Drag vertically or horizontally to rotate continuously, including past the poles where vertical rotation previously stopped.

    The saved documentation preview uses the JavaScript bundle served by this local docs site. Reexecuting the notebook uses your locally built frontend.
