- **Dataset:** 76,356 cells imaged in 3D with MERFISH through about 100 µm of
  tissue depth. Colors show cell clusters.
- **Controls:** drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Select a cluster or gene to
  explore its distribution through the tissue; see the
  [CellCloud guide](../visualizations/cell-cloud.md).
- **Data:** from [Three-dimensional single-cell transcriptome imaging of thick tissues](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/90029v1),
  prepared as [point-cloud DegaFiles on GitHub](https://github.com/broadinstitute/scverse-owkin_thick_merfish/tree/main/landscape_files).
- **In a notebook:** the [CellCloud Thick MERFISH](../examples/brief_notebooks/Landscape-3D_thick_MERFISH.ipynb)
  example links the same CellCloud to a Clustergram of cluster signatures.
